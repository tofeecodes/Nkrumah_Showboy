Who We Are

A story too big for one stage

Nkrumah Showboy brings Kwame Nkrumah's life and legacy to the stage through original music and performance. It's produced by Bambu Heritage Productions — the Ghanaian company behind a decade of Heritage Theatre work — built by theatre-makers, historians, musicians and producers who believe this story deserves to be told by ourselves, for the world.

Authentic storytelling

Original music, real locations, and a cast telling Ghana's founding story on its own terms.

Built to last

A dedicated theatre space and artist residencies — this production is the beginning, not the whole story.

A national moment

Staged as part of Ghana's 70th independence anniversary — a story for the whole country to gather around.

Made for the world

From Nkroful to Accra, then touring locally, continentally, and internationally.

Nkrumah Showboy started with a simple question: why hasn't Ghana told this story on this scale before? Kwame Nkrumah's life carries everything great musical theatre is built on — ambition, love, betrayal, and a vision big enough to change a continent. We're building a production worthy of that story, and an organisation that outlasts any single show: a home for Ghanaian musical theatre, artist residencies, and work that tours the world.

Our Journey

From WogbeJeke to Nkrumah Showboy

Bambu Heritage Productions has spent over a decade bringing African history to the stage. Nkrumah Showboy is the newest chapter in that story.

2015

WogbeJeke: Our Journey launches

Chief Moomen founds Bambu Heritage Productions' flagship Heritage Theatre series — a multimedia epic of drama, music, dance and poetry chronicling Ghana's history from ancient times to today.

2017

Ghana's official 60th anniversary presentation

WogbeJeke is selected as Ghana's official theatre presentation for the nation's 60th independence anniversary celebrations.

2018

Zoom Zoom: Once Upon a Champion

The Heritage Theatre series turns to boxing legend Azumah Nelson's life story, yet to be staged in Accra and in his hometown of Bukom.

2023

The Mansa World launches at UNESCO, Paris

A 500-performer theatrical exploration of Mansa Musa and the West African empires previews at UNESCO headquarters on Africa Day.

2025

UNESCO partnership & Mansa Musa and The Trail of Lost Gold premiere

Bambu Heritage Productions formally partners with UNESCO to promote the General History of Africa programme, as "Mansa Musa and the Trial of Lost Gold" premieres at the National Theatre of Ghana.

2026 – 2027

Nkrumah Showboy Now

Our newest production: performing at the national Ghana@70 commemoration in Nkroful (Sept 2026), premiering in Accra (Oct 2026), and staged in full for the flagship 70th anniversary production (March 2027).
